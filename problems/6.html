$$\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin(2x)^3\cos x\ dx$$

Solution

Look that \begin{align*} A &= \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin(2x)^3\cos x\ dx = \int_{0}^{\pi/2} (2\sin x\cos x)^3\cos x\ dx = 8\int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^3 x\cos^4 x\ dx \\ &= 8\int_{0}^{\pi/2} (1-\cos^2 x)\cos^4 x\sin x\ dx = 8 \int_{0}^{\pi/2} (\cos^4x-\cos^6x)\sin x\ dx. \end{align*} Now let $u =\cos x \Rightarrow du = -\sin x$, we have \begin{align*} A&=-8\int_{1}^{0}u^4-u^6\ du = 8\int_{0}^{1} u^4-u^6\ du = 8\left[\dfrac{1}{5}u^5 - \dfrac{1}{7}u^7\right]^1_0 = \dfrac{16}{35}. \end{align*}
