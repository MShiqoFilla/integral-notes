$$\int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin (x)\sin (2x)\sin (3x)\ dx$$

Solution

By applying some trigonometric identities, we have \begin{equation*} \sin(x)\sin(2x)\sin(3x) = \sin x \cdot (2\sin x\cos x) \cdot (3\sin x-4\sin^3 x) = (6\sin^3 x - 8\sin^5 x)\cos x. \end{equation*} Now set $u = \sin x$, we have \begin{align*} \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin (x)\sin (2x)\sin (3x)\ dx &= \int_{0}^{\pi/2} (6\sin^3 x - 8\sin^5 x)\cos x\ dx \\ &= \int_{0}^{1} (6u^3 - 8u^5)\ du = \left[\dfrac{3}{2}u^4-\dfrac{4}{3}u^6\right]^1_0 = \dfrac{1}{6}. \end{align*}
