$$\int_0^1 x^{13/2}\sqrt{1+x^{5/2}}\ dx$$

Solution

Let $u=x^{5/2} \Leftrightarrow x = u^{2/5} \Rightarrow dx = \frac{2}{5}u^{-3/5}\ du$. Now we have \begin{align*} \int_{0}^{1}x^{13/2}\sqrt{1+x^{5/2}}\ dx=\dfrac{2}{5}\int_{0}^{1}u^{13/5}\sqrt{1+u}\cdot u^{-3/5}\ du = \dfrac{2}{5}\int_{0}^{1}u^2\sqrt{1+u}\ du. \end{align*} Now set $y=1+u \Rightarrow du = dy$ we get \begin{align*} \int_{0}^{1}x^{13/2}\sqrt{1+x^{5/2}}\ dx &= \dfrac{2}{5}\int_{0}^{1}u^2\sqrt{1+u}\ du = \dfrac{2}{5}\int_{1}^{2}(y-1)^2\sqrt{y}\ dy \\ & = \dfrac{2}{5}\int_{1}^{2}y^{5/2}-2y^{3/2}+y^{1/2}\ dy = \dfrac{2}{5}\left[\dfrac{2}{7}y^{7/2}-\dfrac{4}{5}y^{5/2}+\dfrac{2}{3}y^{3/2}\right]_1^2 \\ &= \dfrac{8}{525}\left(11\sqrt{2}-4\right). \end{align*}
