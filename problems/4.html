$$\int_0^\infty \ln\left( \frac{e^x+1}{e^x-1} \right)\,dx$$

Solution

Set $u=e^{-x} \Rightarrow dx = -1/u\ du$, we have \begin{align*} \int_{0}^{\infty} \ln\left(\dfrac{e^x+1}{e^x-1}\right) dx &= \int_{0}^{\infty} \ln\left(\dfrac{1+e^{-x}}{1-e^{-x}}\right) dx = \int_{1}^{0}\ln\left(\dfrac{1+u}{1-u}\right) \cdot -\dfrac{1}{u}\ du \\ &= \int_{0}^{1}\dfrac{1}{u}\ln\left(\dfrac{1+u}{1-u}\right)\ du = \int_{0}^{1} \dfrac{1}{u}\left(\ln(1+u)-\ln(1-u)\right)\ du. \end{align*} By series representation of $\ln(1+u)=u-\dfrac{u^2}{2}+\dfrac{u^3}{3}-\ldots$, \begin{align*} \int_{0}^{\infty} \ln\left(\dfrac{e^x+1}{e^x-1}\right) dx &= \int_{0}^{1} \dfrac{1}{u}\left(\left(u-\dfrac{u^2}{2}+\dfrac{u^3}{3}-\ldots\right)-\left(-u-\dfrac{u^2}{2}-\dfrac{u^3}{3}-\ldots\right)\right)\ du\\ &= \int_{0}^{1}\dfrac{1}{u}\left[2\left(u+\dfrac{u^3}{3}+\ldots\right)\right]\ du=2\int_{0}^{1}\left(1+\dfrac{u^2}{3}+\dfrac{u^4}{5}+\ldots\right)\ du\\ &=2\left[u+\dfrac{u^3}{3^2}+\ldots\right]^1_0=2\left(1+\dfrac{1}{3^2}+\dfrac{1}{5^2}+\ldots\right)=2\cdot \dfrac{\pi^2}{8}=\dfrac{\pi^2}{4}. \end{align*}
