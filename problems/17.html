$$\int_{-1}^1x^{2025}\ln\left(1+e^x\right)\ dx$$

Solution

Set $u= -x \Rightarrow dx = -du$, we get \begin{align*} A &= \int_{-1}^1x^{2025}\ln\left(1+x\right)\ dx = \int_{1}^{-1}-u^{2025}\ln\left(1+e^{-u}\right) \cdot -du = -\int_{-1}^{1}u^{2025}\ln\left(\dfrac{1+e^{u}}{e^u}\right)\ du\\ &= -\int_{-1}^{1}u^{2025}\ln\left(1+e^u\right)du + \int_{-1}^1u^{2025}\ln e^u\ du = -A + \int_{-1}^1u^{2026}\ du. \end{align*} Therefore, \begin{align*} 2A = \int_{-1}^1u^{2026}\ du \Leftrightarrow A = \dfrac{1}{2}\left[\dfrac{u^{2027}}{2027}\right]^1_{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{2027} = \dfrac{1}{2027}. \end{align*}
