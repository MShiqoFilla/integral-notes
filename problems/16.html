$$\int \dfrac{x^{-1/2}}{1+x^{1/3}}\ dx$$

Solution

Let $x=u^6 \Rightarrow dx = 6u^5\ du$. We have \begin{align*} \int \dfrac{x^{-\frac{1}{2}}}{1+x^{\frac{1}{3}}}\ dx &= \int \dfrac{u^{-3}}{1+u^2} \cdot 6u^5\ du = 6\int\dfrac{u^2}{1+u^2}\ du = 6\int \left(1-\dfrac{1}{1+u^2}\right)\ du \\ &=6\left(u-\arctan u\right) + C = 6\left(\sqrt[6]{x} - \arctan\sqrt[6]{x}\right) + C. \end{align*}
