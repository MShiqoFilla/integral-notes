$$\int_1^\infty\dfrac{\{x\}}{x^2}\ dx$$

Solution

\begin{align*} \int_1^N \dfrac{\{x\}}{x^2}\ dx &= \sum_{k=1}^{N}\int_k^{k+1} \dfrac{\{x\}}{x^2}\ dx = \sum_{k=1}^{N}\int_k^{k+1} \dfrac{x-\lfloor x\rfloor}{x^2}\ dx = \sum_{k=1}^{N}\int_k^{k+1} \dfrac{x-k}{x^2}\ dx \\ &= \sum_{k=1}^{N}\left[\log x+\dfrac{k}{x}\right]_k^{k+1} = \sum_{k=1}^{N}\left(\log\dfrac{k+1}{k}+\dfrac{k}{k+1}-1\right) = \sum_{k=1}^{N}\left(\log\dfrac{k+1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}\right) \\ &= \log\prod_{k=1}^{N}\dfrac{k+1}{k} + 1 - \sum_{k=1}^{N}\dfrac{1}{k} = 1 - \left(\sum_{k=1}^{N}\dfrac{1}{k} - \log (N+1)\right) \\ &= 1 - \left(\sum_{k=1}^{N+1}\dfrac{1}{k} - \log (N+1)\right) + \dfrac{1}{N+1} \end{align*} Now taking limits $N\rightarrow \infty$ and by definition of the famous Euler-Mascheroni constant: $\gamma$ we get \begin{align*} \int_1^\infty \dfrac{\{x\}}{x^2}\ dx = 1-\lim\limits_{N\rightarrow\infty}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{N+1}\dfrac{1}{k} - \log (N+1)\right) + \lim\limits_{N\rightarrow\infty}\dfrac{1}{N+1} = 1-\gamma. \end{align*}
