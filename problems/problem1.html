$$\int_1^\infty\dfrac{1}{e^{x+1}+e^{3-x}}\ dx$$

Solution

This is kinda straightforward, \begin{align*} \int_{1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{e^{x+1}+e^{3-x}}\ dx &= \dfrac{1}{e^2} \int_{1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{e^{x-1}+e^{1-x}}\ dx = \dfrac{1}{e^2} \int_{0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{e^{x}+e^{-x}}\ dx = \dfrac{1}{e^2} \int_{0}^{\infty}\dfrac{e^x}{e^{2x}+1}\ dx \\ &= \dfrac{1}{e^2} \int_{0}^{\infty}\dfrac{d(e^x)}{(e^{x})^2 +1}\ = \dfrac{1}{e^2} \cdot \tan^{-1}(x) \Bigr|_1^{\infty} = \dfrac{1}{e^2}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) =\dfrac{\pi}{4e^2}. \end{align*}
