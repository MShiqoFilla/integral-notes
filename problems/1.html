$$\int\dfrac{dx}{1+2x^2+x^4}\ dx$$

Solution

Set $x=\tan u \Rightarrow dx = \sec^2u\ du$ we have \begin{align*} \int \dfrac{dx}{1+2x^2+x^4} &= \int\dfrac{dx}{(x^2+1)^2} = \int \dfrac{\sec^2u}{(\tan^2u + 1)^2}\ du = \int \dfrac{\sec^2 u}{\sec^4 u}\ du = \int \cos^2u\ du \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2}\int 1+\cos 2u\ du= \dfrac{1}{2}\left(u+\dfrac{1}{2}\sin2u\right) + C = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(u+\sin u\cos u\right) + C. \end{align*} Since $x=\tan u$ we know that $\sin u = \dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}$ and $\cos u = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}$, therefore \begin{align*} \int \dfrac{dx}{1+2x^2+x^4} = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\arctan x + \dfrac{x}{1+x^2}\right) + C. \end{align*}
